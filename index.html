How to Play

1

Open the Bot. Click the Play on Telegram button above to open the official Jutebingo bot in Telegram. No additional registration form is required.

2

Choose a Game Room. Browse available game rooms and select one that suits you. Rooms are organized according to player availability.

3

Select Your Bingo Cards. Each card contains a 5×5 number grid. Choose your preferred cards and participate according to the room's game rules.

4

Follow the Number Calls. Numbers are announced one by one through audio calls. Matching numbers are automatically highlighted on your cards.

5

Complete a Winning Pattern. Follow the game instructions and complete the required row, column, or diagonal pattern to qualify as a winner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No additional application is required. Jutebingo operates through Telegram. Simply open the bot using your Telegram account.

Can I play for free?

Free game options may be available to eligible players. Check the bot for current room availability, entry requirements, and applicable game rules.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. You can share your invitation link with friends and invite them to join Jutebingo. Referral rewards, if available, are subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

Which languages are supported?

Jutebingo supports Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, and English, including audio number announcements.

How are numbers selected?

Numbers are selected randomly by the game server. All players participating in the same round receive the same number calls. Winning patterns are checked automatically according to the game rules.

Who can play Jutebingo?

Jutebingo is intended for adults aged 18 and above. Please check local laws and regulations before participating. Play responsibly.
